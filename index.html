    Without the PC: from an Instagram export file

    How to get the export from Instagram (about 2 minutes)
    1. In Instagram open your profile, then the menu, then Accounts Center.
    2. Go to Your information and permissions, then Download your information (it may be called Export your information).
    3. Choose Download or transfer information, pick your Instagram account, then Some of your information.
    4. Tick only Followers and following, then Next.
    5. Choose Download to device. Date range: All time. Format: JSON. Then Create files.
    6. Instagram tells you when the file is ready, usually within minutes. Download it. It is a ZIP file.
    7. Come back here, press Load my Instagram export and choose that file.
