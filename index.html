Turn on notifications

Get a notification on this phone whenever one of your projects sends a message, even when the app is closed.

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Claude News from your home screen and tap the button below.

    No messages yet. The feed shows the last 12 hours from your projects plus everything that was pushed to this phone.
